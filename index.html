Open-field defence · Sector 7 campaign

Ironfield Vanguard

There are no roads out here. Hostiles cross open ground from every bearing, each column marching on a different site. You drive the only rover in the sector: haul supply, deploy defences where you stand, and race between outposts before the next wave breaks.

Versus mode

Two commanders, two identical sectors. Both of you face the same neutral waves, every 45 s and growing. Spend supply on raids that land in your rival's sector, and every raid you send permanently raises your income. The first HQ to fall, or the first to lose both outposts, loses.
